Construction Support Centre launched ahead of Rs 2 trillion budget

23 Nov 2026 | By Sindy Fernando

Sri Lanka has launched the ‘Builders’ gateway’ Construction Industry Support Centre to address administrative bottlenecks, project delays and other challenges affecting the construction sector, as the Government prepares to implement a Rs 2 trillion (Rs 2,000 billion) development budget for 2027, the Department of Government Information (DGI) said on Friday (20).
Against the backdrop of the Rs 2,000 billion development budget presented for 2027, the Builders’ Gateway will establish a direct coordination mechanism between construction industry stakeholders and government agencies to address existing bottlenecks and facilitate the implementation of development projects, the DGI said. 
The centre and its official online platform were launched under the patronage of Housing, Construction and Water Supply Minister Susil Ranasinghe and Secretary to the President Dr Nandika Sanath Kumanayake. It operates under the National Enterprise Facilitation Center (Viyasa) and the direct supervision of the Presidential Secretariat.
Speaking at the launch, Ranasinghe said approximately 95% of the work involved in implementing government development projects was carried out by the private sector, highlighting the construction industry's significant role in delivering public-sector development initiatives, according to the DGI.
He noted that the centre would enable small, medium and large-scale businesses to seek assistance in resolving issues relating to tender and procurement procedures, contractual matters and other administrative processes, the DGI stated.
The services will be available free of charge to construction industry stakeholders, including builders, suppliers, machinery providers, service providers and consultancy firms. They can access the services either by visiting the centre or through its online platform, according to the DGI.
