JAT joins portfolio spanning John Keells, Aitken Spence, Dialog, Tokyo Cement, Lion Brewery and others

Sri Lankan equity portfolio reported to exceed $ 60 m

Global assets under management at $ 7.55 b as at 30 Sept.

UK-based global equity investment manager Hosking Partners LLP has increased its exposure to Sri Lankan equities with the purchase of a 5.18% stake in JAT Holdings PLC for Rs. 1.05 billion. It bought the entire holding of the company’s second-largest shareholder, Falcon Trading Ltd.

Funds managed by Hosking Partners bought 27,077,498 shares at Rs. 38.8 each. Before the sale, Falcon Trading ranked second only to Aelian Gunawardene, who holds a controlling 63.63% stake in JAT.

The deal adds JAT to a Sri Lankan portfolio that Hosking Partners built largely during the 2023 economic crisis, which made it one of the country’s largest foreign equity investors. The firm is among the top 20 shareholders in John Keells Holdings, Aitken Spence, CT Holdings, Richard Pieris, Bukit Darah, Dialog Axiata, Tokyo Cement, Ceylon Guardian Investment Trust, Teejay Lanka and Lion Brewery. Its Sri Lankan equity portfolio was reported to exceed $ 60 million.

Hosking Partners was founded in 2013 by British investor Jeremy Hosking. It had approximately $ 7.55 billion in assets under management as at 30 September, 2026, according to its fund factsheet. It invests in international equity markets through institutional mandates and pooled investment funds. The firm takes a long-term, contrarian approach and uses capital-cycle analysis, which assesses how changes in supply within an industry affect companies’ returns on capital over time.

JAT Holdings is a Sri Lankan multinational with market-leading positions in industrial wood coatings in Sri Lanka and Bangladesh. Its acquisition of New Zealand-based Mirotone has expanded the Group’s international manufacturing and commercial presence, including in Australasia.

In the six months to 30 September, 2026, JAT’s Group revenue grew by 36% year-on-year, revenue from foreign operations rose by 115% and Group profit after tax increased by 27%. The company reported net assets of Rs. 23.48 a share as at that date.
