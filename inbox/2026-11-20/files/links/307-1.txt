The Port of Colombo recorded a 10% increase in transshipment volumes during the first ten months of 2026 compared with the same period last year. Throughput rose from 5,825,909 twenty-foot equivalent units (TEUs) between January and October 2025 to 6,406,363 TEUs during the corresponding period in 2026. This represents an increase of 580,454 TEUs.

Transshipment volumes also increased in October 2026. The port handled 680,125 TEUs during the month, compared with 620,830 TEUs in October 2025. This represents year-on-year growth of 9.6%, with an additional 59,295 TEUs handled during the month.

Reflecting this sustained growth, the Port of Colombo advanced to 20th place among the world’s busiest container ports in the first half of 2026, according to the latest Alphaliner rankings, moving up from 26th place at the end of 2025.

This achievement further strengthens Colombo’s standing as a leading transshipment hub in the Indian Ocean, supported by its strategic location, extensive international shipping connections and continued investment in port infrastructure and terminal capacity. Ongoing expansion and modernisation initiatives are expected to further enhance the port’s operational capabilities, accommodate growing container volumes and strengthen its position as a competitive maritime gateway connecting major global trade routes.
