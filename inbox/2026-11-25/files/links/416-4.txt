SL awaits final IMF board approval on 2 Dec

24 Nov 2026 

The International Monetary Fund (IMF) Executive Board has scheduled 2 December, to consider Sri Lanka’s 2026 Article IV Consultation and Seventh Review under its Extended Fund Facility (EFF) arrangement. 
The session will also cover requests for waivers of non-observance of performance criteria, modifications of performance criteria, and a financing assurances review. 
Executive Board approval will release approximately $ 345 million in fresh funding, bringing total disbursements under the four-year $ 3 billion facility to roughly $ 2.7 billion.
