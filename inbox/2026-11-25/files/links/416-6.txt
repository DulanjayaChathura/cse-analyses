The Cabinet of Ministers has approved obtaining a $ 30 million policy-based loan from the OPEC Fund for International Development to support Sri Lanka’s ongoing economic stabilisation, recovery and structural reform program.

The financing, offered following a positive assessment of the Government’s reform program and policy commitments, is intended to complement financial assistance provided by the World Bank under its Development Policy Operation.

The proposed facility comprises $ 24 million from the OPEC Fund’s Ordinary Capital Resources and a further $ 6 million from its Special Capital Resources.

The Government’s broader reform program aims to restore macroeconomic stability, strengthen fiscal sustainability and establish the foundations for sustainable and inclusive economic growth.

The proposal to this effect was submitted by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake, in his capacity as the Finance, Planning and Economic Development Minister.
