SL’s overseas missions generate Rs 6.6 b in revenue

24 Nov 2026 | By Sindy Fernando

Sri Lanka’s overseas diplomatic missions have generated Rs 6.6 billion (Rs 6,600 million) in revenue over the past period through services provided by Embassies and High Commissions, Foreign Affairs Minister Vijitha Herath said in Parliament yesterday (23).
Herath said: “Through the services provided by the Ministry, facilitated directly via our Embassies and High Commissions, we have generated an income of Rs 6,600 million over this past period.”
The Ministry of Foreign Affairs’ Consular Affairs Division provides services to the general public, including document attestation and authentication, assistance to Sri Lankans overseas, repatriation of stranded citizens, and support in obtaining compensation and other entitlements for Sri Lankans.
In the same parliamentary remarks, Herath also outlined the country’s foreign remittance performance, projecting that total remittances would reach a record of $ 9 billion, surpassing the $ 8.1 billion received last year.
Herath added: “As of the end of October 2026, $ 7.6 billion have already been received as remittances to the country. With an average monthly inflow of approximately $ 750 million, we can expect at least another $ 1.5 billion during the months of November and December. This would bring the total for this year to 9 billion dollars, surpassing last year’s figures and marking a new high for foreign remittances, which would be a historic achievement.”
Herath highlighted the Government’s efforts to improve Sri Lanka’s image internationally had helped restore the confidence of expatriate Sri Lankans, encouraging them to send their earnings back home.
“In reality, this achievement has been made possible because we, as a nation, have elevated our image before the global community and strengthened the confidence that Sri Lankans living abroad have in our country,” he said.
